About

Researching efficient thermal-fluid systems

I am a mechanical engineering researcher with expertise in heat transfer, fluid mechanics, thermal management, experimental aerodynamics, and computational fluid dynamics. My doctoral research investigated singular and oscillating jets impinging on surfaces across subsonic to supersonic flow regimes.

My current research interests include advanced turbine cooling, aerospace thermal management, wind-tunnel aerodynamics, flow separation mitigation, active and passive flow control, turbomachinery, and sustainable thermal-energy technologies.

Research

Selected research themes

Jet Impingement Cooling

Experimental and numerical studies of circular, annular, inclined, and oscillating jets for high-performance cooling.

Oscillating Jets

Fluidic-oscillator-based heat transfer enhancement using intrinsic unsteadiness without moving parts or external actuation.

Wind-Tunnel Aerodynamics

Closed-test-section wall corrections, blockage effects, pressure distributions, separation, and airfoil/cylinder testing.

High-Speed Flows

Subsonic-to-supersonic impinging jets, compressibility effects, shock structures, and thermal-fluid interactions.

CFD & Validation

RANS/URANS modelling, turbulence-model assessment, mesh independence, and validation against infrared and flow diagnostics.

Thermal Management

Aerospace and turbomachinery cooling, flow control, electronics cooling, and sustainable energy-system applications.
